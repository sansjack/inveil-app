# Trademarks

The Inveil source code is licensed under the [Apache License 2.0](LICENSE). That licence covers the code and documentation. As its section 6 says, it does not grant permission to use the project's names, logos or trademarks.

The "Inveil" name and the Inveil logo (the veil and four-point star mark in [`docs/assets/logo.svg`](docs/assets/logo.svg)) are not licensed for use by forks or derived products.

## What you may do

- Use, modify and redistribute the code under Apache-2.0.
- Say truthfully that your software works with Inveil or implements the Inveil backend contract, for example "compatible with the Inveil contract v1", as long as it does not suggest endorsement.
- Redistribute official, unmodified binaries and release assets with their names, signatures and checksums intact.
- Use the name in articles, reviews, talks and discussions about the project.

## What you may not do

- Name a fork, a modified build or a competing product "Inveil", or something confusingly similar.
- Use the logo as the logo of a fork or in a way that suggests your build is official or endorsed.
- Distribute modified builds under the official name or signed with the official keys.

## Forking

If you fork the project, rename it and replace the logo. Product strings live in one file, [`crates/inveil/src/brand.rs`](crates/inveil/src/brand.rs), and [docs/RELEASING.md](docs/RELEASING.md) explains how to use your own signing keys and update feed. Keep the copyright and licence notices required by Apache-2.0.

If you are unsure whether a use is allowed, open an issue and ask.
